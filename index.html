Compatible kits

Works over Bluetooth with ELK-BLEDOM, Triones / Happy Lighting and Govee family controllers. Kit not recognized? The app runs a short test with you to identify it.

Which app for ELK-BLEDOM kits on iPhone?

Most budget car interior LED strips and footwell kits show up in the Bluetooth list as ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLEDOB, LEDBLE or LEDCAR. Car Ambiance controls them directly on iPhone: colors, white, the kit's built-in effects plus the app's 14 effect types, brightness, speed and schedules. On kits with a built-in microphone, the lights can follow your music.

Which app for Triones / Happy Lighting kits on iPhone?

Kits built on a Triones controller appear as Triones, LEDBlue, QHM or Dream in the Bluetooth list and are usually sold with the Happy Lighting app. On iPhone, Car Ambiance connects to them directly and gives you colors, a separate white channel, the controller's built-in effects with speed control, and the app's zones and schedules.

Which app for Govee kits on iPhone?

Govee Bluetooth controllers, including the H6114, H61140 and H7090 car interior lights, show up as Govee_ or ihoment_ in the Bluetooth list. Car Ambiance talks to them over Bluetooth on iPhone: colors, white, the kit's own scenes and, on RGBIC models, a different color for each segment. No Wi-Fi and no account needed.

Car Ambiance is an independent app and isn't affiliated with these brands.

Frequently asked questions

Which kits work?

Kits that use ELK-BLEDOM, Triones or Govee Bluetooth controllers. If your kit is not recognised, the app can walk you through identifying it.

The app cannot find my kit.

  • Make sure the kit has power. Some kits only power up with the ignition on.
  • Close the kit's original app, because most controllers accept one connection at a time.
  • Turn on Bluetooth and allow the Bluetooth permission for Car Ambiance in your phone's settings.

What happens after the 20-day trial?

You can watch a short video ad to keep using every feature for 24 hours, as often as you like. A plan or the lifetime purchase removes ads and the need to watch them.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Car Ambiance and cancel there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Uyumlu kitler

ELK-BLEDOM, Triones / Happy Lighting ve Govee ailesi kontrolcülerle Bluetooth üzerinden çalışır. Kitiniz tanınmadı mı? Uygulama sizinle kısa bir test yaparak onu tanımaya çalışır.

ELK-BLEDOM kitleri için iPhone'da hangi uygulama?

Uygun fiyatlı araç içi LED şeritlerin ve ayak bölgesi kitlerinin çoğu Bluetooth listesinde ELK-BLEDOM, ELK-BLEDOB, LEDBLE ya da LEDCAR adıyla görünür. Car Ambiance bunları iPhone'da doğrudan yönetir: renkler, beyaz, kitin kendi efektleri ve uygulamanın 14 efekt türü, parlaklık, hız ve zamanlayıcılar. Dahili mikrofonu olan kitlerde ışıklar müziği takip edebilir.

Triones / Happy Lighting kitleri için iPhone'da hangi uygulama?

Triones kontrolcü kullanan kitler Bluetooth listesinde Triones, LEDBlue, QHM ya da Dream adıyla görünür ve genellikle Happy Lighting uygulamasıyla satılır. Car Ambiance iPhone'da bunlara doğrudan bağlanır: renkler, ayrı beyaz kanalı, kontrolcünün kendi efektleri ve hız ayarı, uygulamanın bölge ve zamanlayıcı özellikleri.

Govee kitleri için iPhone'da hangi uygulama?

H6114, H61140 ve H7090 araç içi ışıkları dahil Govee Bluetooth kontrolcüleri Bluetooth listesinde Govee_ ya da ihoment_ adıyla görünür. Car Ambiance iPhone'da bunlarla Bluetooth üzerinden konuşur: renkler, beyaz, kitin kendi sahneleri ve RGBIC modellerde her segmente ayrı renk. Wi-Fi ya da hesap gerekmez.

Car Ambiance bağımsız bir uygulamadır, bu markalarla bağlantısı yoktur.
